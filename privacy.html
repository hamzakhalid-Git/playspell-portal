PlaySpell portal: privacy

The PlaySpell portal is a private tool for a team that makes playable ads. Only Google accounts its admins have added can sign in.

What it uses from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, the portal receives your email address and name, and checks the email against its list of people. It doesn't ask Google for anything else: no contacts, files, mail or calendar.

What it keeps

  • For each person on the portal: their email, name, role and team, and when they were added.
  • The builds people publish, their settings and translations, and share links.
  • A record of changes and who made them, for the portal's admins.
  • Where Unity is signed in, by computer name, so it can be signed out.

Sign-in sessions are kept only in your browser tab. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Share links

Anyone with a share link can play that playable without signing in. A link can be switched off at any time.

Removal

An admin can remove you from the portal; you then can't sign in, here or in Unity. Ask your portal's admin to remove your details.
